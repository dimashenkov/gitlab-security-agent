Three hallucination layers, not one. report_finding rejects a finding whose evidence does not exist verbatim in the file — before it becomes a finding. The other two are adversarial: the finding is handed back with a mandate to refute it. That is why unclear is a real verdict — a steady “cannot tell” costs less than a different guess every run.
